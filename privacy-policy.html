Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: January 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

At Lotus365 (accessible via lotus365slot.com), safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of our visitors and registered players is a core operational commitment. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, the lawful basis for handling it, how we use and store it, and the controls available to you regarding your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal, necessary data required to deliver high-performance, secure web entertainment:

  • Direct Inquiries: When you contact support or submit general inquiries, we collect your name, email address, and message contents.
  • Technical & Log Data: Like most web servers, our systems automatically log IP addresses, browser user-agents, device categories, referring pages, and timestamp requests to prevent fraud and DDoS attacks.
  • Cookies & Local Preferences: We use lightweight local session storage to retain user UI preferences (such as sound settings, dark theme parameters, or game filter selections). We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site advertising trackers.

3. How We Use Collected Information

Information collected on lotus365slot.com is utilized exclusively for:

  • Responding to player questions, technical troubleshooting, and customer service inquiries.
  • Monitoring platform availability, optimizing page load performance, and maintaining system integrity.
  • Detecting unauthorized intrusion attempts, phishing clones, bot abuse, and anomalous behavior.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and responsible gaming safety standards.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertising brokers. Data may only be shared under the following conditions:

  • Essential Infrastructure Providers: Web hosting servers (e.g., Hostinger) and network routing utilities necessary for serving encrypted web traffic.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: If mandated by valid law enforcement requests, legal subpoenas, or to prevent demonstrable fraud.

5. Data Security Standards

All data transmitted between your browser and lotus365slot.com is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Our server configuration includes standard security headers such as X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff and X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN to prevent clickjacking and MIME-type sniffing.

6. User Rights & Data Inquiries

You have the right to request clarification on any data associated with your interaction, request data rectification, or request deletion of contact messages submitted via our platform. To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Team at support@lotus365slot.com.
