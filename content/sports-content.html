Understanding Cricket Betting Odds: Back vs. Lay

To assist players in understanding cricket exchange dynamics, Lotus365 provides transparent explanations of exchange terminology:

  • Back Betting (Blue Odds): Wagering on an outcome to occur (e.g., backing Team India to win the match).
  • Lay Betting (Pink Odds): Wagering against an outcome occurring (acting effectively as the bookmaker against that selection).
  • Session Odds & Fancy Markets: Real-time wagers on specific over boundaries, fall of wickets, or individual player strike rates.
